# Building Types

## Civic/Social

* Tent -> House -> Villa -> Mansion (nobility quarters)
    * +citizen cap
    * higher versions provide more "stuff" (states like happiness/karma)
        * higher levels enabled based on citizens count
        * higher levels provide more protection from negative happenings
* Church -> Cathedral
    * +happiness/karma
    * +luck (as praising the lord helps)
* Pub
    * +happiness/karma
* Workshop
* park -> theater -> colosseum
* Town Hall/Center

## Economy/Resources

* field -> farm -> plantation
    * +food prod
* Granary
    * +food cap, +rat protection
    * protection from happenings (not basic granary, but more advanced silo lowers probability of rats happening)
* "big granary":
    * resistince to happenings
* Mine
    * +gold prod, +people upcome (needs people to work)
* warehouse
    * stores multiple resource types (food + construction resources)
* market -> trading post -> stock exchange

### Trading

* Marketplace
    * +gold prod (per people), -trade price modifier
* Harbor

## Military

* Barracks
    * +army cap, -army buy mod, +att/def mod
* Archery
    * +army cap, -army buy mod, +att/def mod
* Stable
    * +army cap, -army buy mod, +att/def mod
    * horses (allowing knights?)
* Smithy/Armoury
    * "improve stuff"?
* Wall /"defence palisades"
    * +defense
    * less likely to get raided by bandits
