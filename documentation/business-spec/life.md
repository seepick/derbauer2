# Life

* player age/children/death/reincarnation concept (like in fugger2)
* the user has a certain age in years
    * start with being 16 years (tell a story)
    * with earliest 20, can increase user.title

## Death

* once reach higher age, increase probability to die
* after 40 or so, depends on how long game should last

## Mating and Reincarnation

* during game, can find mating partner, marry, have children (if lucky and well-behaved)
* once dead, "reincarnate" (if tech researched)

## Birthday

* celebrate birthday once a year (52 weeks), receive presents
* depending on "fame" state (karma, citizen's happiness), change reward amount
