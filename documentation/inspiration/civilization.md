# Civilization

* PLAY: https://classicreload.com/play/civilization.html
* start: difficulty (chief,...), number NPCs, tribe (roman, babylonian,...), name
* every once in a while, upgrade your castle
    * something shiny for the eye, choose style, expand grow (village view)
* Official Handbook: https://www.retrogames.cz/manualy/DOS/Civilization_-_Manual_-_PC.pdf

## Buildings

Palace - Reduces corruption, required for capital
Barracks - Veteran land units
Granary - Stores food
Temple - Makes unhappy citizens content
Marketplace - +50% tax/luxury income
Library - +50% science output
Courthouse - Reduces corruption
City Walls - Triples defense vs. non-artillery units
Aqueduct - Allows city growth beyond size 10
Bank - +50% tax/luxury (requires Marketplace)
Cathedral - Makes 4 unhappy citizens content
University - +50% science (requires Library)
Colosseum - Makes 3 unhappy citizens content
Factory - +50% production
Manufacturing Plant - +50% production (requires Factory)
Stock Exchange - +50% tax/luxury (requires Bank)
Sewer System - Allows city growth beyond size 20
Research Lab - +50% science (requires University)
Harbor - +50% food from ocean squares
Police Station - Reduces unhappiness from military units

# World Wonders

Pyramids - Granary in every city
Hanging Gardens - Makes 1 content citizen happy in every city
Colossus - +1 trade in every square producing trade
Lighthouse - +1 movement for sea units, safe sea travel
Great Library - Free advances discovered by 2+ other civs
Oracle - Temple effect in every city
Great Wall - Doubles defense vs. barbarians, +1 to defense negotiations
Sun Tzu's War Academy - Barracks in every city
King Richard's Crusade - Cathedral in every city
Marco Polo's Embassy - Embassy with all civilizations
Michelangelo's Chapel - Cathedral in every city
Copernicus' Observatory - +50% science in one city
Magellan's Expedition - +2 movement for sea units
Isaac Newton's College - +50% science in one city

# Units (ATK/DEF/MOV)

Militia - Basic defense unit (1/1/1)
Phalanx - Early defensive unit (1/2/1) - Requires Bronze Working
Legion - Roman heavy infantry (3/1/1) - Requires Iron Working
Musketeers - Gunpowder infantry (2/3/1) - Requires Gunpowder
Pikemen - Anti-cavalry unit (1/2/1) - Requires Feudalism
Cavalry - Mounted unit (2/1/2) - Requires Horseback Riding
Knights - Heavy cavalry (4/2/2) - Requires Chivalry
Crusaders - Mounted warriors (5/1/2) - Requires Chivalry (special)
Cavalry (modern) - Fast attack unit (5/3/2) - Requires Tactics
Catapult - Siege weapon (6/1/1) - Requires Mathematics
Cannon - Artillery (8/1/1) - Requires Metallurgy
Artillery - Modern artillery (12/2/2) - Requires Robotics
Chariot - Early mobile unit (3/1/2) - Requires The Wheel
Horsemen - Mounted raiders (2/1/2) - Requires Horseback Riding

Non-Military:

Diplomat - Espionage and diplomacy (0/0/2) - Requires Writing
Caravan - Trade unit (0/1/1) - Requires Trade
Spy - Advanced espionage (0/0/3) - Requires Espionage (Civ I didn't have Spies - this was Civ II)
